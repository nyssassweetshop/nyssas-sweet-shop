Nyssa's Sweet Shop — order-enabled Netlify website

Deploy:
1. In Netlify, open the existing Nyssa's Sweet Shop site.
2. Open Deploys and drag the entire extracted folder (not just index.html) into the manual deploy area, or use the site's deploy upload option.
3. In Site configuration / Forms, enable form detection if prompted.
4. After the deploy completes, open Forms. Confirm that "bakery-orders" appears.
5. Go to Forms > Submission notifications > Add notification > Email notification.
6. Enter your email address and complete the confirmation email Netlify sends to that address.
7. Submit a test order from your live website and confirm it appears in Forms and reaches your inbox.

The form collects customer name, phone, optional email, treat, quantity, date needed, and details.
Customer orders are requests only, not confirmed until you reply.
